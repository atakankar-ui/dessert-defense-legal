🧁 Dessert Defense

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Dessert Defense is a mini game on TikTok, made by ati's game project ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: we don't collect any personal information about you.

What the game saves

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of game data:

  • your coins, stars, unlocked levels, machines and upgrades;
  • your music and sound on/off choices;
  • the day you last received a reward for returning to the game.

This data is stored on your device using TikTok's storage for mini games. It is not sent to us, and we can't see it. It contains no name, contact details, location or other personal information.

What we don't do

  • We don't have player accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • We don't run our own servers, analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell or share any information about you.
  • The game has no in-game purchases.

TikTok

Dessert Defense runs inside the TikTok app, and TikTok provides the features it uses: saving, sound, and the options to add the game to your home screen or open it from your TikTok profile. TikTok handles the information it collects according to its own Privacy Policy. For these features, the game only learns whether an action worked (for example, whether you returned through your home screen icon), never who you are.

Optional ads

The game may offer optional video ads, for example to triple the coins from a level. Ads are never forced: you choose whether to watch one. The ads are provided by TikTok, which may use information as described in TikTok's Privacy Policy. The game only learns whether an ad was watched to the end, so it can give you the reward.

Children

Dessert Defense is available to people who are allowed to use TikTok under TikTok's rules. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing we hold that we could show, change or delete. Your game progress stays in TikTok's storage on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
